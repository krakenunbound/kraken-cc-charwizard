# Changelog

## 1.0-beta2 (2026-10-19)

Reported on Discord: the Adventurer's Backpack was loaded and its gear and spells showed up, but none of its classes could be chosen.

### Every book you load is used
- Races and classes now come from **every C&C book you load**, not only the Players Handbook. The Source column shows which book each one is from, and the Source filter narrows the list.
- All 17 Adventurer's Backpack classes can be built: Arcane Thief, Archer, Bard (Spell Caster), Divine Knight, Ethereal Knight, Foresworn, Magic User, Oathsworn, Pacer, Paladin (Spell Caster), Primal Druid, Ranger (Spell Caster), Rune Mark, Seeker, Skald, Thief and Warrior Priest.
- A class from another book is read from its own **Advancement** table: hit die, prime, experience, base to hit, hit dice and spells per day, plus the book's "13+" experience rule.
- The "Spell Caster" ranger, paladin and bard start from the Players Handbook class (base to hit, hit dice) and take experience and spells per day from their own table.

### Adventurer's Backpack spell casters
- Warrior Priest and Paladin (Spell Caster) cast from the Cleric list, Ranger (Spell Caster), Bard (Spell Caster) and Primal Druid from the Druid list, and the Rune Mark from its runes.
- Spells per day that the Backpack keeps in the class text (Warrior Priest, Primal Druid, Rune Mark) and bonus spells for a high attribute are included.
- The Rune Mark's runes go on the sheet's Spells tab (the ruleset makes no spell block for it).

### Levels
- Where the ruleset's class loader cannot set the level (the Spell Caster variants, and any level past a book's printed table), the wizard sets it: level, base to hit, class abilities, experience for the next level and caster level. This works both when creating a character and with **Lvl Up**.
- Above the printed tables (13th level and up), the Castle Keeper's total hit points and base to hit now reach the sheet (they were lost before, leaving 0 hit points).

### Name
- The wizard is now called **C&C Character Creation Wizard** (title bar, tooltip, extension list and chat), without "Official": it is a fan-made extension, not an official product.

### Layout
- Hit Points page at 13+: the note, the two boxes and the total no longer overlap.
- Class page at 13+: one clear line about the Castle Keeper's hit points and base to hit.
- A class without a portrait (the Backpack classes) uses the full width of the class card.
- The Race and Class instructions say the lists hold every book you have loaded.

### Known limits
- The Backpack classes have no class portrait yet.
- Magic User and Divine Knight have no spell list in this ruleset.
